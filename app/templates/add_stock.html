How it works:

  • Enter a valid stock symbol (e.g., AAPL for Apple)
  • Specify the number of shares you own
  • Choose the date you want to start tracking from
  • The app will fetch the stock price on that date
  • You'll see the total market value change (shares × price) up to today

Example:

  • 100 shares of AAPL on 2025-01-01
  • Price on 2025-01-01: $150
  • Initial Value: 100 × $150 = $15,000
  • Current Price: $160
  • Current Value: 100 × $160 = $16,000
  • Gain: $1,000 (6.67%)
